Snowballz @VERSION@ for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 10.13+)
==============================================================

Drag Snowballz.app to your Applications folder and open it.

The game is not signed by Apple, so the first time macOS refuses to open
it.  Then:
  * macOS 15 or newer: System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down,
    click "Open Anyway" next to Snowballz, and confirm.
  * older macOS: right-click (Ctrl-click) Snowballz.app > Open > Open.
  * or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Snowballz.app

Everything the game needs (SDL2, music) is inside the app.
Settings:      ~/.snowballz.ini
Crash reports: ~/.snowballz_crash.log  (please send it with bug reports)
Online games: Online > Host a game.  The game opens TCP port 5555 on your
router by itself (UPnP) and the lobby shows the address to give your
friends, with a Copy button.  They choose Online > Join a game and paste it
(Ctrl+V).  If the router can't do it automatically, the lobby says how to
forward the port by hand.
The first time you host, macOS asks whether Snowballz may accept incoming
network connections, and (macOS 15 and newer) whether it may find devices
on your local network: click Allow for both (the second one is how it
talks to your router).
Controls: see README.md.

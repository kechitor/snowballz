## New graphics

Almost every picture in the game is redrawn from the original Blender files,
sharp on today's screens.

### Penguins
- **Sharper penguins:** every penguin animation is rendered at 4 times the
  old size.
- **Brighter team colours:** the outfits and flags are brighter, so it's
  easier to tell the teams apart.
- **Frozen penguins:** frozen workers and frozen snowballers now look
  different from each other.
- **Gathering crystal:** workers swing a pick while they gather crystal.

### The map
- **Sharper scenery:** trees, ice pinnacles, igloos and crystals are
  sharper, and an empty crystal now shows as pyramids.
- **Fish holes:** the fish holes are newly painted.
- **Markers:** the markers for your orders are redrawn.
- **Snow walls:** walls are rebuilt from rounded snow bricks and clear ice,
  with battered tiles that are missing bricks or have a chunk broken off.
- **Ground:** the snow and ice are sharper when zoomed in. The ice has faint
  skate scratches, hairline cracks and specks of frost.
- **Effects:** snowballs, their dents in the snow and splats on the ice,
  footprints, chimney smoke, falling snowflakes, the selection box and the
  frozen penguin's flag are redrawn sharp.
- **Footprints:** footprints are shaped like the penguins' flat feet and
  point the way they walk.
- **Power-ups:** the power-ups and the countdown's orb are redrawn sharp, as
  glossy badges.

### Menus
- **Full-screen backgrounds:** the menu, loading, winner and loser screens
  fill screens up to 21:9, with a cloudy sky and a redrawn SnowballZ logo.
- **Map pictures:** every map's picture is sharper.
  - Small Crystal Rush has a picture for the first time.
  - White Forest's statue now stands in a forest.
- **Game icon:** a frozen worker on a midnight sky.
- **Interface:** sharper buttons and text fields with rounded corners, a new
  icicle mouse cursor, a wooden easel under the map pictures, and a
  sharper igloo in the online lobby.

### In the game
- **New HUD:** rounded glass panels with a thin icy edge in place of the
  flat boxes.
- **What you have:** your fish, crystal and penguins, top left.
- **Command bar:** the selection panel is now a bar along the bottom:
  pictures of the selected workers and snowballers with their numbers
  (click one to keep only those), their warmth, and buttons for Stop (O),
  Snow wall (B), Ice wall (U) and Change class (X).
- **Power-ups:** the countdown has a ring that fills up until the drop.
- **Crystal race, alerts, minimap and squads** are restyled to match, and
  "Players and alliances" is a button as well as the P key.

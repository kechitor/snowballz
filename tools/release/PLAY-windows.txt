Snowballz @VERSION@ for Windows (64-bit)
==================================

Start the game: double-click snowballz.exe.
Keep SDL2.dll and the data, maps and plugins folders next to it.

The first time Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (the game is
not signed): click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Online games: Online > Host a game.  The game opens TCP port 5555 on your
router by itself (UPnP) and the lobby shows the address to give your
friends, with a Copy button.  They choose Online > Join a game and paste it
(Ctrl+V).  If the router can't do it automatically, the lobby says how to
forward the port by hand.
The first time you host, Windows Firewall asks about snowballz.exe: click
"Allow access".  If friends still can't get in, open Windows Security >
Firewall & network protection > Allow an app through firewall, and tick
Snowballz for the kind of network you are on (Private or Public).

Settings:      %APPDATA%\SnowballZ.ini
Crash reports: %APPDATA%\SnowballZ_crash.log  (please send it with bug reports)
Controls: see README.md.
